# Value Proposition Generator Skill
Generate a value proposition based on the representative persona.

## Rules
- Answer in Vietnamese
– Do not invent impossible facts.
– Keep all attributes internally consistent.
- The value proposition must have pain points, wishes and customer jobs.
- Each pain point, wish and job must be corresponding to the pain reliever, gain creator, product and services.
- Just read the representing persona in `persona/personacreation.md` to generate value proposition.
- Do not think about a solutions, just generate the value proposition.
